Where most engagements start

The Commercial Diagnostic

A focused assessment of your sales organisation: pipeline health, win rates, forecast accuracy, process, team capability and enterprise readiness, typically completed in two to three weeks.

I begin by listening: to the problem as you see it, and to the people closest to it. I then present a clear diagnosis and a practical plan, in writing and in a working session. You leave knowing precisely what to fix first, whether or not we continue to work together.

What you get

  • A structured review of your sales motion, pipeline and forecast
  • A written report setting out findings and priorities
  • A working session to agree the plan
  • A clear recommendation on next steps

Fees are determined by scope. Get in touch and I will prepare a proposal.

Questions

Questions clients often ask first

What is a Commercial Diagnostic?

A focused assessment of your sales organisation, pipeline health, process, team and enterprise readiness, typically completed in two to three weeks. It concludes with a written report, a working session to agree the plan and a clear recommendation on next steps, whether or not we continue to work together.

Who do you work with?

Startups, SMEs and established companies that sell a considered, high-value product or service, where trust, multiple stakeholders and a disciplined process determine the outcome. Most are building a sales function, fixing or scaling an existing one, or moving upmarket to enterprise buyers.

Where do you work?

I work from London and Lagos. From London, I serve companies across the UK, Europe and beyond, including the United States and the Gulf; from Lagos, companies across Nigeria and the wider African continent. Engagements are delivered in person or remotely, as each requires.

How are fees set?

Fees are determined by scope. Projects carry a fixed or staged fee, fractional leadership is held on a monthly retainer, and training is priced per programme. Get in touch and I will send a proposal.

Do you train teams or do the work?

Both, depending on what the business requires. Some engagements build the strategy, process and playbooks; others train and coach the team on live pipeline; fractional engagements provide senior commercial leadership for an agreed number of days each month. Every engagement concludes with your team running it.

How many clients do you take on?

A limited number at any one time, so each receives senior attention. The Commercial Diagnostic also serves as a test of fit before any larger commitment.
