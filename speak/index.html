Invite Ajoke to speak

Ajoke accepts a limited number of invitations each year to speak, moderate or host fireside conversations at events where business, finance, technology and policy leaders meet. Her talks draw on a decade of selling to the most exacting buyers in financial markets, and on her advisory work with growth companies. She is also invited to host book launches and conversations on parenting and motherhood.

Topics

What Ajoke speaks about

Most talks return to a single thread: how companies win and grow complex, high-value B2B revenue. Each can be tailored to the audience and the format.

  1. 01

    Selling to the most demanding buyers

    What a decade of selling data and technology to tier 1 and tier 2 banks and global asset managers teaches any company about trust, timing and process.

  2. 02

    From founder-led sales to a sales function

    When founders should step back as principal salesperson, and how to hand over selling without losing momentum.

  3. 03

    Going upmarket

    How growth companies, including those from Africa and other emerging markets, secure their first enterprise and global clients.

  4. 04

    Listen first, then lead

    What a counselling education taught her about commercial leadership, coaching and high-stakes conversations.

  5. 05

    Ambition and motherhood

    Honest reflections on building a commercial career while raising a family, and the support that makes both possible.

How invitations work

Four steps, each handled personally

  1. 01

    Invitation

    Share the event, the date, the audience and the role you have in mind for Ajoke.

  2. 02

    Conversation

    Where there is a good fit, a short call follows to understand the room and what success looks like.

  3. 03

    Briefing

    Ajoke prepares for the audience, the speakers and the questions that matter to them.

  4. 04

    On the day

    Ajoke arrives prepared, keeps to time, and leaves the room with something to act on.
