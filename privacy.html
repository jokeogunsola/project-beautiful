Legal

Privacy notice

Last updated October 2026. This notice explains what happens to the information you send through this website, in plain English.

Who is responsible for your information

This website belongs to Ajoke Tella, a commercial leader and advisor working from London and Lagos. Ajoke Tella is the controller of the personal information you send through this site.

Privacy contact: ajoke@ajoketella.com.

What is collected

Enquiry form: your name, work email and company, and anything optional you add: your role, what you are looking for, company stage, how you heard about Ajoke and your message.

Speaking and hosting invitation form: your name, email, organisation, optional role, what you would like Ajoke to do, the event name, and any optional details you add about the date, city, audience and event.

Insights sign-up: your email address and, if you choose to give it, your first name.

Please do not include sensitive personal information (for example health, religion or political opinions) in your message.

Why it is used, and the legal basis

  • To reply to your enquiry or invitation. Legal basis: legitimate interests in responding to a request you have made.
  • If you decide to work together, to prepare a proposal and enter into an agreement. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract.
  • To keep records the law requires, such as contracts and accounts. Legal basis: legal obligation.
  • To email you new insights, if you have subscribed. Legal basis: your consent, given by ticking the box on the sign-up form. You can withdraw it at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any email or by emailing the privacy contact above.

Your details are not sold or shared for marketing. Only people who tick the box on the insights sign-up form are added to the insights mailing list. No decisions are made about you by automated means.

Where it goes

Form submissions are passed to Ajoke's email inbox through Netlify Forms, which acts on Ajoke's instructions under a data processing agreement. Insights sign-ups are also collected through Netlify Forms. If a dedicated newsletter service is later used to send insights, this notice will be updated to name it before any email is sent through it.

International transfers. Ajoke works between the United Kingdom and Nigeria, and service providers may store data in other countries, including the United States. Where information is transferred outside the UK, the European Economic Area or Nigeria, it is protected by appropriate safeguards, such as adequacy regulations, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or the transfer conditions in the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

How long it is kept

Enquiries and invitations are kept for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and for no more than two years if no work follows. Insights subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe. If you work together, records are kept for as long as the law requires for contracts and accounts, usually six years in the UK.

How it is protected

The website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Access to enquiries is limited to Ajoke and protected by strong passwords and two-step sign-in.

Cookies and visitor statistics

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It uses Plausible Analytics to count visits and see which pages and buttons are used. Plausible does not set cookies, does not collect personal data and does not follow you across other websites, so no cookie banner is needed.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to ask for access to the information held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable format. To use any of these rights, email the privacy contact above. You will receive a reply within one month.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority:

  • United Kingdom: the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
  • Nigeria: the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).
  • European Union: the data protection authority in the country where you live or work.

Children

This website is for businesses and professionals. It is not intended for children, and information from children is not knowingly collected.

Changes to this notice

If this notice changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
